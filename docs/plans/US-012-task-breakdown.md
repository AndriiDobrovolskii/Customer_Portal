## Notes

- T1/T2 run in parallel — neither depends on the other, per the standard rule.
- **Correction (2026-09-01, caught during T2 execution):** `app/core/security.py`, `app/core/cache_keys.py` (`perm_epoch_key`), `app/core/revocation_cache.py` (`PermissionEpochCache`), `app/core/config.py`, and `.env.example` were originally assigned to T2 (data-layer-builder) on a misapplied precedent — re-checking `docs/plans/US-007-task-breakdown.md`'s actual note shows US-2.3's `core/security.py` addition (`hash_refresh_token`) landed under its **service** task (T4), not its data-layer task (T2); `data-layer-builder`'s own skill definition is explicit that it may only create/modify a module's own `models.py`/`repository.py`/`cache.py`, never `app/core/*`. All five files moved to T6, whose `users/service.py` work is their actual first consumer.
- T6 is scoped separately from T4/T5 even though `service-and-router-builder` is the same execution skill, because it modifies a **different module's** existing `service.py` (`users`, not `roles`) for the cross-cutting integration point FR-2 requires (`perm_epoch` check, `scopes` claim at token issuance), plus the `app/core/*` primitives that integration needs. Keeping it a distinct task makes the cross-module blast radius (impact-analysis's flagged `users` ↔ `roles` dependency) visible in the sequence rather than buried inside T4's roles-only scope.
- The FR-7 cross-module exception (roles' repository reading `users.status` directly, user-approved 2026-09-01) lives in T2's `UserRoleRepository`, not T6 — it's a repository-layer read, not a service-to-service call, so it doesn't touch `users/service.py` at all.
- The 8 items carried forward from spec-review/API-design as unresolved (404 for unknown target, empty-roles-array, duplicates, If-Match absent/stale, check-precedence order, initial-ETag acquisition, role-to-scope mapping [now resolved, see below], admin-bootstrap dependency) are **not** separate tasks. Role-to-scope mapping was resolved 2026-09-01 (minimal per-persona) and is now T3's seed data. Empty-array and duplicate-role-name behavior were resolved 2026-09-01 (plan-review finding, user-confirmed): both reject with `422 validation-failed`, reusing FR-4's existing unknown-role rejection mechanism — T4 implements this directly rather than choosing a default of its own. The remaining unresolved items (404 for unknown target, If-Match absent/stale, check-precedence order, initial-ETag acquisition, admin-bootstrap dependency) stay genuinely open and are left for T4/T5 to make a documented, conservative choice on as written, or for a follow-up story.
- `tests/conftest.py`'s role-seeding fixture is scoped to T8 (integration), not a separate task, matching the US-2.4 precedent of folding `conftest.py` changes into whichever test task first needs them.
